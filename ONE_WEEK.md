# One week of homeschool without buying more curriculum

A free starter from [Homeschool IRL](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/store/homeschool-irl).

Use what you already have: a library card, a kitchen, a walk, one book, and the public web. This is not a thin link dump. It is a week you can actually run.

**Time box:** 2-3 focused hours a day, plus life. If the day blows up, keep the 20-minute sprint and call it a school day.

**Rule:** one meaningful activity can cover reading, writing, math, science, and life skills. Do not write five separate lesson plans.

Printables and full frameworks live on TPT. This page does not replace them and does not give those files away.

## Before Monday (20 minutes)

Write four lines on paper. That is your curriculum for the week.

1. What this child will actually do without a fight
2. One skill that is slipping (reading stamina, number sense, writing a complete thought)
3. One real-life job that has to happen anyway (meals, laundry, a sibling, a pet, a budget)
4. What done looks like on Friday — one sentence

If you are brand new, the paid companion is [Your First 30 Days of Homeschooling](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Your-First-30-Days-of-Homeschooling-30-Steps-to-Launching-Your-Adventure-11027334). If you already own a pile of unused boxes, start with [Stop Buying Curriculum](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Stop-Buying-Curriculum-Build-Your-Own-Homeschool-17557044) instead of another order.

Attendance / hours log if your state wants paper: [Homeschool Attendance Tracker](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Homeschool-Attendance-Tracker-Printable-Fillable-PDF-Attendance-Log-17598930). A notebook dated each day is enough for this free week.

## The daily skeleton

Same shape every day so you are not reinventing school at 8 a.m.

| Block | Minutes | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Start | 10 | Read aloud or they read. No worksheet. |
| Anchor | 45-90 | One activity from the day below. |
| Skills bite | 20 | The slipping skill. Timer on. Stop when it rings. |
| Life | happens | Cook, walk, sort mail, fix something. Name the subjects out loud once. |
| Close | 5 | What did you figure out? One sentence in a notebook. |

When they refuse the plan, drop to a 20-minute sprint and a choice of two tasks. That toolkit, written out, is [Reluctant Learner Homeschool Toolkit](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Reluctant-Learner-Homeschool-Toolkit-Learning-Sprints-Reset-Tools-17557086).

## Free resources this week actually uses

Public, no account required unless noted. Use the library app if you have one.

**Books and text**

- Local library + Libby / Hoopla (library card)
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) — public-domain books
- [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/) — cleaner public-domain editions
- [CommonLit](https://www.commonlit.org/) — free texts and questions (free account)

**Math**

- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) — pick one course, one unit, not a binge
- [Illustrative Mathematics](https://illustrativemathematics.org/) — free problem tasks
- Kitchen scale, measuring cups, a receipt from the grocery run

**Science / world**

- [NASA Climate Kids](https://climatekids.nasa.gov/)
- [Smithsonian Learning Lab](https://learninglab.si.edu/)
- Window, backyard, grocery produce, weather app

**Writing**

- Notebook. That is the resource.

**ASL add-on (optional, 10 minutes)**

- [Lifeprint / ASL University](https://www.lifeprint.com/)
- Paid 30-day loop: [Beginner ASL Activities](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Beginner-ASL-Activities-30-Days-of-American-Sign-Language-for-Homeschool-17599700)

Do not open five new platforms. Library + Khan + one science site + a notebook is a full week.

## Monday — one book, many subjects

Read a chapter. Child tells you what happened in five sentences. Sort ten words. Estimate a distance. Find the setting on a map. Same Khan unit all week for the skills bite.

Method with 20 ready anchors: [The One-Activity Homeschool](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/The-One-Activity-Homeschool-Turn-One-Activity-into-Multiple-Subjects-17556974).

## Tuesday — kitchen is the classroom

Cook or prep one real meal. Double or half a recipe. Cost it from a receipt. Rewrite the steps. Ask why it browned or rose. Clean as you go.

Longer treatment: [Life Skills Are Curriculum](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Life-Skills-Are-Curriculum-Real-Life-Learning-Activities-for-Homeschoolers-17557006).

## Wednesday — walk, weather, and a question

Walk or watch the window. Note weather. Sketch three things. Ask one question you cannot answer on the sidewalk. Look it up after you get home.

If Wednesday is a fight: [How to Homeschool a Reluctant Learner](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/How-to-Homeschool-a-Reluctant-Learner-When-Your-Child-Resists-Learning-17556928) and the [bundle](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Reluctant-Learner-Bundle-Homeschool-Edition-17557340).

## Thursday — make something that has to work

Repair, flyer, prototype, or a $20 dinner list. State the problem, list constraints, test on a person, change one thing.

Alternatives to packets: [Homeschooling Without Worksheets](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Homeschooling-Without-Worksheets-Simple-Alternatives-for-Real-World-Learning-17557063).

## Friday — show the week, then stop

Ten-minute show-and-tell. You write three lines: learned, resisted, inherited by next week.

Parent framework: [The Simple Homeschool Method](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/The-Simple-Homeschool-Method-A-Practical-Framework-for-Homeschool-Parents-17556839). Four-guide stack: [Homeschool Made Simple Starter Bundle](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Homeschool-Made-Simple-Starter-Bundle-Back-to-School-17557288).
